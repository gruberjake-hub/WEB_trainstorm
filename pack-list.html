Do This First
blockers — don't skip
  • Find your driver's license or state ID — right now, today Must be REAL ID compliant (star in the upper corner) or you need your passport. No ID = no flight. Check your wallet.
    Required
  • Get laundry soap and do laundry You need 4 nights of clothes, clean. Do this as soon as possible — don't leave it for Tuesday night.
    Required
  • Download music to your phone — Spotify or Apple Music Airplane wifi is expensive and unreliable. Download playlists NOW while you're on wifi so they play offline. Great for takeoff if you're nervous. Bring headphones.
    Smart
🎒
What to Pack
per person, in a backpack
  • 4 days of casual clothes — shorts, t-shirts, one light layer Key Largo is hot. You do not need much. It's shorts weather the whole time.
  • One nicer outfit for dinner (one night we're going somewhere decent) Nothing formal — just not a tank top and basketball shorts. One outfit between your four days of clothes is fine.
  • 2 swimsuits each You'll be in the water. One wet swimsuit is miserable. Two means you always have a dry one.
  • Flip flops + one pair of closed-toe shoes Flip flops for everything. Closed-toe for the boat and walking on uneven surfaces.
  • Underwear and socks (4–5 days worth)
  • Sunscreen — SPF 50, a big bottle The Keys sun will destroy you. This is not optional. Reapply constantly on the water.
    Important
  • Sunglasses + hat or cap
  • Headphones For the flight. Download your music before you go.
  • Phone charger + portable battery pack if you have one
  • Any personal medications — in your backpack, not checked If you take anything regularly, it goes in your carry-on. Checked bags can get lost.
  • Dramamine (motion sickness) — one each We're doing a glass bottom boat over the reef. Take it 30–60 min before boarding, not after. Available at any drugstore.
  • Cash — about $60–80 A few places work better with cash. ATMs in the Keys are sparse.
🧳
Goes in the Shared Checked Bag
coordinate with Jake
  • Toiletries over 3oz — shampoo, conditioner, body wash, etc. Liquids over 3oz can't go through security in a carry-on. Consolidate into the checked bag.
  • Bug spray Mangroves at dusk = no-see-ums. Bring it.
  • Snorkel mask, snorkel, and fins — per person We're snorkeling off the beach at Rock Reef. Cheapest move: grab a set at the Walmart in Homestead on the drive down from MIA (~$20–30 each). Or rent from a local shop. Either way, don't expect the resort to have gear available.
    For the water
