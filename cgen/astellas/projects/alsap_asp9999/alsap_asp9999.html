Purpose and Stakeholder Review

Safety Profile Summary

The overall safety profile for this asset as of the signing of this ALSAP draws upon data obtained from 412 participants who participated in clinical trials. The most frequently encountered adverse events associated with ASP9999 include nausea, fatigue, headache and transaminase elevation of which the most common that were considered serious include transaminase elevation meeting Hy's Law criteria.

The benefit-risk profile of ASP9999 is favorable provided the additional hepatic monitoring described in the risk management plan is in place. Across 412 participants the observed transaminase elevations were reversible and detected by scheduled monitoring in every case; the SMT judges the residual risk acceptable for the proposed indication on that condition, and will re-assess when the Phase 3 dataset matures.
