Election Observer Field Card

Central Count · Wisconsin Admin. Code ch. EL 4 · Trust, but verify.
Scroll for the full card. Tap the number at the bottom to call the hotline. To print the fold-up version, use your browser's Print.

On arrival

  • Sign in, show photo ID, get your observer badge/tag.
  • Record the time you arrive and leave.
  • Stand where marked (tape/cones) — 3–8 ft from tables.
  • Speak only to the Designated Election Official (DEO) — don't engage the workers.
  • Texting OK. Leave the room to take a call.
  • No campaign materials. Don't speak to the press.
  • If you step away, ask another observer to cover your area.
  • Give your notes to the team lead before leaving.

Capture every time

  • Exact time (a.m./p.m.)
  • Location — table / ward / station
  • Voter name & number, ward number (when applicable)
  • What you saw — actions, not conclusions
  • Who — worker/inspector name(s) + wristband color
  • Witnesses present
  • Recorded on the inspectors' EL 104?
  • Objection? What you said, to whom, response
  • Evidence + who holds it

Main issues to watch

  • No absentee ballots received/accepted after 8:00 p.m.
  • Two bi-partisan inspectors reconstructing/remaking ballots — verify the remake number on both ballots.
  • Certificate-envelope defects (see box) — should be rejected.
  • Envelope name/address not on the voter list → should not be counted.
  • Names improperly added to the voter list (should not happen).
  • Clerk initials missing on envelope → still counted, but must be recorded on the EL 104. Note it.
  • Record the time ballots are distributed to tables by an official.
  • Do not record passwords/log-ins on tabulators, computers, or machines.
Certificate envelope — when it's incomplete:
  • Voter signature: any mark counts (X, print, scribble). Legibility not required. No date required.
  • Witness signature/printed name: signed OR printed is fine (either one). Neither → incomplete, cannot process.
  • Witness address: needs street number + street name (or "same"/ditto). P.O. Box not acceptable. City-only or zip-only → cannot count.

Recording rules

  • Photos/recording of the location are allowed — central count is not a polling place. EL 4.05(6)
  • Not during password/log-in entry on machines.
  • Hidden videotaping is illegal in WI unless you notify the person.
  • Audio recording is OK only if you are part of the conversation.

Your access & rights

  • Observe all counting. EL 4.05(6)
  • Observation area 3–8 ft from check-in/announce, registration, and remake tables. EL 4.03(1)(b)
  • Observe rejected certificate envelopes. EL 4.03(7)
  • Observe poll lists (minus confidential parts). EL 4.03(8)
  • Official must repeat a name/address once on request. EL 4.03(6)
  • You may not handle original documents EL 4.03(9) or view confidential info. EL 4.03(10)
  • One warning before removal; removals must be documented & reported to WEC. EL 4.03(11)

Conduct

  • Don't initiate conversation with voters. EL 4.04(13)
  • No discussing candidates, parties, or ballot questions. EL 4.04(11)
  • No electioneering; no candidate/party names displayed. EL 4.04(9)-(10)
  • Keep conversation minimal and quiet. EL 4.04(8)

Quick field notes

See a main issue or anything major? Find a team leader — then record everything.
