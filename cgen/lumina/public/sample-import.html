Coaching a missed deadline

Use this page when a teammate missed a date and you still need the work, the relationship, and a next step.

Before you speak

Write the situation, the behavior, and the impact on one sticky note. If it does not fit, you are still arguing with yourself.

What to say

  1. Name the moment: yesterday’s 3pm client send.
  2. Name what you observed: the deck arrived after the call started.
  3. Name the impact: the sponsor left without a decision.

Do

  • Ask how it landed
  • Agree one recovery date

Don’t

  • Stack three older grievances
  • Use always / never

Open the practice prompt

Keep the meeting to ten minutes.

End with a written next step.
