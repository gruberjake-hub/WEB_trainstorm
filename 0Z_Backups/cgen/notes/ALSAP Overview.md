SLIDE 3: What is ALSAP?
Visual Design:
•	Three-component visual breaking down ALSAP conceptually
•	Icons for: Plan | Team | Analysis
On-Screen Text:
What is ALSAP?
ALSAP is three things:
1. A Plan
A documented strategy for when and how we'll analyze aggregate safety data for a specific drug candidate (asset)
2. A Team
Dedicated statistical programmers and statisticians who conduct these analyses
3. A Process
A systematic approach that connects planning, execution, and decision-making
Audio Script:
So what exactly is ALSAP? It's actually three things working together. First, it's a plan—a documented strategy that spells out when and how we'll look at aggregate safety data for each asset. Second, it's a team—we now have dedicated programmers and statisticians whose job is to conduct these analyses. And third, it's a process—standard procedures that connect the planning, execution, and decision-making. Together, these elements give us a proactive, systematic way to understand the safety profile of our drug candidates as data accumulates.
SLIDE 4: ALSAP vs. Other Safety Processes
Visual Design:
•	Comparison table showing ALSAP, SMT, SAC, and Trial-Level Monitoring
On-Screen Text (Comparison Table):
Process	What It Does	Who's Involved	Key Distinction
ALSAP	Systematic analysis of aggregate safety data	Dedicated programmers & statisticians	Proactive, planned analyses
SMT	Strategic safety oversight & decision-making	Cross-functional team	Governance, not analysis execution
SAC	Unblinded review for regulatory decisions	Specialized committee	Event-triggered, not routine
Trial-Level	Real-time safety monitoring within one trial	Trial team, investigators	Single trial scope, often blinded
Audio Script:
You might be wondering: how does ALSAP fit with everything else we do for safety? Here's the key distinction. Trial-level monitoring happens in real-time within each study, often with blinded data. The Safety Management Team—or SMT—provides strategic oversight and makes governance decisions. The Safety Assessment Committee—SAC—steps in when we need unblinded review for regulatory decisions. ALSAP is different: it's the systematic, proactive analysis of aggregate data across trials. ALSAP provides the analytical foundation that feeds into SMT discussions and, when needed, SAC reviews. Each plays a distinct role in our overall safety strategy.

SLIDE 6: How ALSAP Works: The Basic Flow
Visual Design:
•	Process flow diagram with 5 steps (left to right): Plan → Assign → Execute → Review → Act
On-Screen Text:
The ALSAP Workflow
Step 1: Plan
Asset team determines ALSAP is needed → Drafts an ALSAP Plan (FORM-AST-34037)
Step 2: Assign
ALSAP programmer/statistician assigned to the asset
Step 3: Execute
ALSAP team extracts data, performs aggregate analyses per plan
Step 4: Review
SMT reviews ALSAP outputs → Assesses for safety signals
Step 5: Act
If findings warrant action: Document, escalate to SAC if needed, implement changes
Audio Script:
Let's walk through how ALSAP actually works. First, the asset team determines that ALSAP is needed and drafts a plan that defines what analyses will be done and when. Second, an ALSAP programmer or statistician is assigned to execute that plan. Third, the ALSAP team extracts data from multiple trials and performs the aggregate analyses. Fourth, the SMT reviews those outputs and looks for safety signals. And fifth, if the findings warrant action—whether that's documenting the results, escalating to SAC for deeper review, or implementing protocol changes—the appropriate steps are taken. It's a systematic cycle designed to catch signals early.
