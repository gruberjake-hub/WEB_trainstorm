Thursday, June 18
Glass Bottom Boat
Day 2 of 5
Where you are
Rock Reef Resort, Key Largo, Florida. On the water, Florida Bay.
  • Morning
    Slow start. Coffee, dock, take your time.
  • 12:15 PM
    Glass bottom boat at John Pennekamp State Park — a 2.5-hour tour over the coral reef. You stay in the air-conditioned cabin and watch the reef through large windows in the hull. No swimming required. One of the great reefs in North America.
  • ~3 PM
    Back to the resort. Rest, sit by the water. The park also has a 30,000-gallon aquarium on-site if you'd like a look.
  • Dinner
    The Fish House — fresh local seafood, one of the best in the Keys.

Today's main event is the boat. Jake will get everyone there and back. You don't need to arrange anything — just enjoy it.
