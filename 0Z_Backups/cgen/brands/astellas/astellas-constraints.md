# Astellas Learning Theme Constraints (v1)

## Tone
- Trustworthy, sincere, visionary.
- Avoid playful/juvenile UI patterns.
- Prefer calm pacing and conservative motion.

## Color usage
- Red/gray are primary.
- Use secondary colors sparingly; ideally one secondary per visualization.
- Keep UI chrome neutral; reserve red for emphasis/active states.

## Typography
- Prefer Arial for digital learning delivery.
- Avoid mixed typefaces and novelty fonts.

## Imagery
- Prefer authentic, real-world workplace/lab settings.
- Avoid staged actors, surreal/CGI science motifs, dramatic lighting.

## Accessibility
- Keyboard-first interactions.
- Visible focus states.
- Maintain strong contrast.
