## Product movement this window

### 1. The coach's job — Kay's "a coach to do what?" is answered

Kay (buyer's-eye friend) probed the weakest point: "build a personal AI coach" was a *mechanism* wearing the costume of an *outcome*. A coach to do **what**? The answer, derived from the existing poles rather than invented:

- **First job — reinstate the *stance*.** The coach's primary purpose is to solve the universal failure mode of transformational programs: the state doesn't survive contact with Monday. The coach re-establishes the participant's **stance** — the low-stakes, curious, "I-can-direct-this" posture — by *re-engaging them in the manner the session did, so the state re-emerges from the interaction* rather than being triggered by recall.

- **"Stance" is now settled, load-bearing vocabulary.** It is the correction term whenever the design drifts toward "re-trigger the feeling." (See Red Lines — this is where integrable-affect protection lives in the coach design.)

- **Tuesday-morning job — see possibility, then make it manageable.** Concretely: the coach surfaces one place AI touches the participant's *actual situated work* (their therapeutic area, review cycle, team friction) that they wouldn't have seen, gives them a low-stakes way to try it, and keeps it small enough to actually do. This is the daily engine of the exit-state promise ("a concrete next step, not a vague intention") and the Stardew-Valley loop as a daily practice.

- **The coach matures along the arc — two modes at two points.**
  - *Generation-first* (entry-state / just-transformed participant): the problem is a **blank** — they don't yet see where AI touches their work. Coach surfaces possibility and grants permission.
  - *Constraint-later* (Stage-2 participant who's now generating): the problem flips to **overabundance** — drowning in half-finished starts. Coach shifts to "carry one to done before opening the next."
  - This is itself a small piece of the still-unmapped arc: **the coach is not static; it changes job as the participant matures.**

- **The overabundance/"swimming" problem is a profile guardrail, not a feature.** One line in the coach's profile: *watch that surfacing possibility doesn't tip the participant into a sea of half-finished starts.* Do not engineer it further.

### 2. Positioning — visceral surface, abstract engine

The single biggest commercial insight of the window, corroborated three ways (Kay's "a coach to do what," Jenn's and Nikki's need for the concrete):

- **The positioning has been living at the altitude of its own architecture** ("belief-layer intervention," "situated world," "fit not correctness," "reinstate the stance"). That language is correct for the **design canon** and probably wrong for the **mouth of the funnel**.
- **Two of the most important inside-track nodes (Jenn, Nikki) are concrete, nuts-and-bolts operators** who can spot value but need it visceral, not abstract. This is not a communication problem to work around twice — it is a **market signal about the beachhead.** The people who source, adopt, and champion inside pharma orgs think this way. If abstraction bounces off Jenn and Nikki, it will bounce off the early buyers too.
- **The correction (D3): lead with the felt result; let the depth be discovered.** This is the discoverability invariant applied to marketing — show them the concrete thing they can see and choose; the philosophy is the pleasant surprise underneath, never the entry fee.
- **Engine vs. dashboard:** the abstraction (stance, situated world, belief layer) is the *engine* and stays in the canon. The Tuesday-morning, thing-on-your-phone, do-it-in-ten-minutes concreteness is the *dashboard.* Early market meets the dashboard.
- **Scope discipline:** the fix is *a sentence, not a system* (D1). Do not rewrite the whole canon into concrete language on spec. Build the visceral surface *from what actually lands* with Jenn/Nikki — field-tested, not pre-engineered.

### 3. The two-forked outcome (confirmed as spine, not scope creep)

The Orchestrator's real target, curriculum-wide:
1. **Condition the participant to thrive in an AI world — whether or not it's with their current company.** (This "whether or not" is the never-withhold / discoverability line expressed at the level of *whose interest the program serves*: it visibly serves the person even if they leave. That integrity is what makes it changeable-belief rather than performed-compliance — and therefore what makes it sellable to the company.)
2. **Position the company to get the most from its people** in bringing the AI revolution inside the walls.

These are the same integrity seen from two sides, not two products. **But:** the *curriculum* aims at both forks; the *4-hour bite* does not have to. The bite must complete **one stage** and leave the participant whole. The org fork needs to be **visible from** the bite (the buyer's second pair of eyes), not delivered **in** it.

- **Open question deferred (correctly):** 4 hours vs. all-day. Do **not** answer by sizing the container to the ambition (D1) — that trades away commercial edge (a 4-hr founder-delivered *intervention* is scarcer, more premium, more bookable, and better-positioned than an all-day *training program*, and halves facilitator load). Seat-time is an **output of the arc map**, not an input. Map the arc first; the duration falls out.

## Red lines (product integrity — flag every time, regardless of temperament)

- **Never withhold to drive sales.** Every chunk delivers full promised value; appetite for the next comes from satisfaction/wholeness, never an engineered gap. In pricing/packaging terms: any move that ration-gates value to force the next purchase is withholding — name it and kill it. (Stage 1 priced as complete; the Role Codex ships fully inside it; it wedges into Stage 3 by being *good*, not gated.)
- **Discoverability invariant.** The transformation must be visible to the participant — something they see and choose, never done to them. If a design choice would work *better* by being less transparent, that's a red line, not a trade-off. (This window: it's also the marketing principle — lead visceral, let depth be discovered — and it's *literally the move that turned Jenn from gate to ally.*)
- **Integrable affect / no therapy register.** This is where "stance" earns its keep in the coach. The coach reinstates the *stance* (cognitive-agentic posture, re-emergent from re-engagement) — it must **never** manufacture or re-trigger *affect* on demand. A device that induces emotional states outside a facilitated container, no one in the room, at unpredictable moments, with no way to metabolize what surfaces — that is uncontained affect delivered by software, and it is *worse* than in-session therapy-drift. State-as-emergent-from-re-engagement is clean; state-as-induced-by-recall is the line.

## Working method — the discipline-tag system

The user works best when avoidance/complexity patterns are caught in real time, but no longer needs each one explained — naming brings the content into his own context. **Convention going forward: flag a discipline that could have gone either way with a quick, vernacular, numbered parenthetical** — e.g. *(D1: overbuild — sentence not system)* — and trust him to unpack it unless he asks. Say more only when a flag is genuinely new or load-bearing to the decision at hand. **Red-line items (above) are the exception — always a full flag, since they're product integrity, not temperament.**

Numbered tags established this session:

- **D1 — Overbuild / 10M solution.** System when a sentence would do; sizing the container to the ambition; building the maximum version before commercial viability is established.
- **D2 — Architecture as avoidance.** Design/analysis motion in the controllable direction while the uncontrollable-but-decisive thing (real outreach, real data) waits. Includes filtering out the uncontrollable thread because it's uncontrollable.
- **D3 — Abstract when visceral is needed.** Climbing back to the intellectually satisfying frame instead of leading with the felt, concrete result. Especially at the mouth of the funnel.
- **D4 — Wrong instrument.** Applying analysis to a question only evidence/data can close. The user's self-identified lifetime failure mode. Resolves by shipping into a real conversation, not by more thinking.
- **D5 — Retreat to the floor.** Max-ID / certain-linear work re-entering dressed as Orchestrator progress ("front door" as the story that makes it feel okay). Distinguish real bounded wedge (fenced, purposeful) from open-ended return (grows to fill all capacity). The tell is whether it's fenced.
