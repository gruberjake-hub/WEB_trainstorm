# Authoring Philosophy

## Unit of Design

The unit of design is **learner state transition**, not content.

We design for:
- psychological posture
- emotional readiness
- cognitive clarity
- agency and trust

Words, visuals, and interactions are *expressions* of intent.

---

## Human Role

Humans provide:
- FROM state → TO state
- business outcomes
- ethical constraints
- brand boundaries
- final approval

Humans do NOT:
- hand-code interactions
- micromanage layout
- wire navigation logic

---

## AI Role

AI acts as:
- instructional architect
- content generator
- structure compiler
- runtime integrator

AI does NOT:
- invent learning goals
- manipulate values
- override declared constraints
- decide what learners “should believe”

---

## Time Without Timelines

Time exists as:
- observable state
- media playback position
- semantic cues

Never as:
- animation tracks
- authoring timelines
- hidden sequencing logic

---

## Design Order (Always)

1. Identify desired learner state
2. Define constraints and risks
3. Propose instructional moments
4. Map moments to components
5. Generate wording last
