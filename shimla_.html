Seats on AA54 — and the split, in that order

The situation: aa.com finds the booking — both names, PE cabin, 787-9 — then greys the map: "seat map unavailable for flights on other airlines, contact British Airways." BA says contact AA. This is policy, not a lookup failure: AA doesn't release advance seats to passengers ticketed by another carrier. The Xs mean not available to you, not taken. The lone open middle seat (15E) is the tell.

1 · Call BA — 1-800-247-9297
Ask for a seat request on the AA-operated segment. Agents can send an interline request. It's a request, not an assignment — treat it as a coin flip. If it lands: split Norah onto her own PNR on the same call; have the agent read back seats on both records before hanging up. If it doesn't: no split.
2 · Thu Sept 25, 8:50 PM Chicago — check in on aa.com the minute it opens
The map unlocks for BA-ticketed passengers at T-24. On one PNR you're a party of two and auto-assign tries to pair you; on two PNRs it doesn't. PE on the 787-9 is 21 seats, 2-3-2. Target an A/C or J/L pair. Everyone else has the same alarm.
3 · Fallback
AA Premium Economy counter at ORD — ask to be seated together.

Why split at all: the extension gets decided in India; once split, your return changes in the app without touching Norah's. But an unsplit booking is not a crisis — one BA call from India splits and changes in the same conversation. Sitting apart for eight hours is the worse outcome. Seats first, split second.

Nerd lever: an ExpertFlyer trial shows the real AA54 seat map for the 26th — tells you whether waiting for check-in is safe or whether to lean on the BA agent.

Monday Sept 28 — Delhi arrival

The hall

Norah's friend's verdict on T3: it's chaos; relax and flow. e-Visa holders have their own immigration line. It moves. Realistic curbside: ~10:00 AM. Show the e-Arrival QR and the printed e-Visa.

Cash

Schwab debit at a bank ATM in arrivals (SBI, HDFC, ICICI, Axis) — not the forex counter. Always choose INR when offered "convert to USD" (that's DCC, a 5–8% tax on your own money). Expect a ₹10,000 per-pull cap; take two. Schwab refunds the fees. Travel notice set before leaving. $100–200 USD as deep backup.

Phone

Verizon TravelPass — $12/day in both the UK and India, 5 GB high-speed/day, charged only on days used. Add it before departure, data roaming ON, it self-activates on a UK tower. ~$120 for the trip as booked. Backup eSIM installed but inactive. WhatsApp stays on the Verizon number.

Jet lag

Do not nap in a hotel bed. Sleeping in the car is fine. Stay up until 9–10 PM local. The 8 AM landing is close to ideal for eastbound adjustment — daylight resets the clock.

How to get a driver here

Plains (Delhi / Chandigarh): Uber Intercity — schedule in-app up to 30 days out, cash accepted. Ola Outstation is the other app. Savaari for a booked driver with English phone support.
Hotels: Taj Chandigarh concierge and Wildflower Hall front desk each arrange a car on an hour's notice. Pricier, zero friction, hotel vouches for the driver. Anything on a hill road: this is the move.
Hills (Shimla): app cabs barely function; the local taxi union at the stand is how it's done. 2026 anchors: Chandigarh↔Shimla sedan ~₹3,000 / Innova ~₹4,500 · Shimla full day ~₹2,500–3,000 · Shimla→Delhi ~₹9–12k. Plus 5% GST, tolls, ₹500–800 night-halt. October isn't peak.

The toy train was cancelled Sept 21 — IRCTC wouldn't cooperate, and there's no version of it worth more phone calls five days out. Consolation: Oct 1 is a full Chandigarh day again, bags travel with you, and the Kalka road is its own kind of scenic.

Dancing

Not obligated. Norah has a formal piece to learn from a video on arrival; you don't. That said, the Baraat is a processional where standing at the edge is the only wrong move — twenty minutes of a bhangra-basics video and four moves (shoulders on the beat, arms up, lightbulb hand, balle balle hop) turns spectator into welcome participant. An hour of bhangra at 8,250 ft is real cardio; hydrate first. Playlist for the commute if you want it: Gallan Goodiyaan · Kala Chashma · London Thumakda · Nachde Ne Saare · Sadi Gali · Mehndi Laga Ke Rakhna. Southall will be playing all of these out of shop doorways — free listening test.

The laptop

The Astellas laptop will connect — the VPN doesn't geo-block — but it's vacation time regardless: Astellas doesn't want people working from foreign jurisdictions for tax-residency reasons, and the e-Tourist visa doesn't permit work, including for Trainstorm. So the laptop's job is triage, not work: keep the inbox from becoming an avalanche, nothing that looks like billable output. A 30-minute morning window at the hotel; in the safe otherwise. If the extension happens, this is the constraint that shapes it — two more weeks is two more weeks of PTO, not two weeks of remote work.

Open decisions

The extension

A week or two for Karan — first contact in about a decade. Decide in India. Blocked on PTO and Karan's availability. The return is changeable for exactly this. Pre-work is the PNR split, sequenced behind seats.

The buffer day

Even without the Karan weeks, one extra day removes the Oct 5 dash — a real night in Chandigarh, arrive Delhi rested. It's a separate question from the extension; ask Norah as two questions, not one. Her call as much as yours.

Practical

Water: bottled only. Payments: cards widely accepted; Apple Pay is not (India); rupees for small stuff. UPI QR codes are everywhere and need an Indian bank account — not yours.
Power: two adapters — between them they must cover UK Type G (Heathrow) and India Type C/D (round pins). Taj/Oberoi rooms usually have universal sockets; the Chandigarh house won't. Details in the pack list.
Altitude: Wildflower Hall is at 8,250 ft, higher than Shimla town. The deck's "modest, loose-fitting clothes" is plains advice. Bring real layers.
Med kit: rehydration salts · Imodium · painkillers · Dramamine (Oct 2 convoy) · prescriptions in original packaging, counted for the long version of the trip. Full list in the pack list.
Timing: everything starts late and runs late. The deck says chill vibes. So does Norah's friend about the airport. Believe both.
