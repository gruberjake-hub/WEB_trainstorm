Why This Matters

Organizations are busy. They are not necessarily intelligent.

Teams generate decisions, knowledge, experiments, and strategy constantly. But most of that intelligence remains siloed, unstructured, and invisible to other teams that need it.

Fragmented memory Meetings happen. Documents accumulate. Decisions get made. But the organization rarely develops a usable memory of what it learned.
Local reasoning Teams make decisions with partial context because nobody can reliably track what related groups are discovering at the same time.
Human resistance Even good AI initiatives stall when people are psychologically unprepared for the identity shift required to work with intelligent systems.
