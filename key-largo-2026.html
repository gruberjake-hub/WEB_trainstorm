Before You Go
📍 Glass Bottom Boat — Book Ahead

The glass bottom boat at John Pennekamp State Park is the trip's centerpiece and it sells out. Reservations at pennekamppark.com or call (305) 451-6300. We're targeting the morning of June 18th.

✈ Katelynn — First Flight

BRING HEADPHONES and make sure to have your list DOWNLOADED TO YOUR PHONE. Good music is essential for a nervous flier. Bring valid government-issued photo ID (driver's license, state ID, or passport) to get through airport security. Make sure it's REAL ID compliant (look for a star in the upper corner) or bring your passport. Arrive at OHARE at least 2 hours before departure. Jake will walk everyone through security.

🌡 Weather

Key Largo in June is warm and humid — mid-to-upper 80s during the day, low 80s at night. Afternoon rain showers are common but brief. Mornings are usually beautiful. Sunscreen is non-negotiable. SHORT are non-negotiable.

Helpful Hints
☀️
The sun is serious
Key Largo is at 25° latitude — the UV index is high year-round and brutal in June. Reapply sunscreen every 90 minutes on the water and especially on the boat. A hat and UV shirt will save you from a miserable sunburned second day.
⛵
Glass bottom boat motion
When the boat anchors over the reef, it rocks with the current while you're looking down through the glass. Some people who aren't normally motion-sick still feel it. Take Dramamine 30–60 minutes before boarding, not after. The sun deck is better than the cabin if you feel queasy — look at the horizon.
🌧️
Afternoon showers are normal
June is the start of rainy season in the Keys. Afternoon showers usually roll through fast — 20–30 minutes and it's over. Don't cancel plans for a 40% chance of rain. Just know it might happen and roll with it.
🐟
Snorkeling etiquette
Don't touch the coral — it's alive and very fragile, and it's also illegal in the sanctuary. Don't stand on the reef. Don't feed the fish. Look, float, enjoy. The reef does all the work.
🦟
Bugs at dusk near the mangroves
No-see-ums (tiny biting midges) come out at dusk near mangrove areas. They're small enough to go through standard screens. Bring bug spray if you're sitting outside at golden hour — it's worth it.
🚗
The drive on US-1
US-1 through the Keys is a two-lane road with lots of traffic lights and slow zones. Don't expect to drive fast — that's not the point. Allow extra time for every trip, especially heading back to MIA on departure day. The scenery is worth it anyway.
💵
Bring some cash
Tacos Jalisco and Harriette's are easier with cash. Several smaller spots along US-1 prefer it or have card minimums. $60–80 cash per person for the trip should cover it.
📵
Cell service is fine, but put the phones down
You'll have service throughout Key Largo. The Keys have a way of making the phone feel irrelevant. Let it.
