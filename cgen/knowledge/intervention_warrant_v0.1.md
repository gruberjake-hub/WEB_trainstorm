# INTERVENTION WARRANT
*Trainstorm Architecture — Step 0*
*Version 0.1 — 2026-04-30*
*Status: First formal encoding — derived from first principles*

---

## Purpose

The Intervention Warrant is the mechanism by which the engine establishes whether a proposed training intervention is justified by the actual value of what is being asked of the learner.

It exists to:
- Protect learners from the deployment of a powerful substrate intervention against an unwarranted target
- Protect designers from interpersonal pressure to proceed without evidence
- Protect Trainstorm's ethical integrity at scale, independent of any individual's judgment or courage in a specific client relationship

The warrant is a **hard gate.** The engine does not proceed without it.

---

## The Three Questions

---

### Question 1: Value Evidence
**What is the honest, human-level case for this intervention?**

Not the business case. Not the regulatory requirement as stated. The human case.

- What gets genuinely better for the learner if this intervention succeeds?
- What gets better for the end beneficiary — patient, customer, stakeholder — through the learner?
- Is there real evidence for this, or is it asserted?

**On regulatory requirements specifically:**

A regulatory requirement is not automatically a fail condition — and it is not automatically a pass condition. Regulations in high-stakes industries frequently exist because people were harmed. The regulation is the crystallized memory of a human cost. The question is whether the initiative has maintained the living connection between the requirement and the human rationale behind it — or whether it has severed that connection and reduced compliance to a checkbox.

*Pass condition: The human case is specific and honest. It would survive scrutiny from a skeptical but reasonable learner. If the value case is regulatory, the connection between the regulation and its human rationale is alive, traceable, and present in how the initiative is being framed.*

*Fail condition: The human case cannot be articulated beyond organizational or regulatory obligation. The initiative is presenting compliance as an end in itself. The learner is being asked to follow a rule without access to why the rule exists.*

*Warranted response to a fail on these grounds: Do not redesign the training. Reconnect the initiative to its human rationale first. Then train. The goal is not learners who comply with rules — it is learners who understand the human cost the rules were designed to prevent, deeply enough that they would invent similar rules themselves if the rules didn't exist.*

---

### Question 2: Adoption Legitimacy
**Is what we're asking of the learner reasonable?**

- Is the system, process, or behavior change actually ready to be adopted?
- Do the learner's working conditions support the adoption being asked for?
- Is the organizational infrastructure in place to sustain the change?
- Are we training people into a fully built reality or a half-built one?

*Pass condition: The ask is reasonable, the infrastructure exists, and the learner has a genuine path to successful adoption.*

*Fail condition: The system isn't ready, the support isn't there, or the ask is structurally unreasonable given actual working conditions. Training will not fix this.*

---

### Question 3: Cynicism Audit
**What does the organizational sediment honestly look like around this initiative?**

- What prior initiatives has this population experienced that are similar to this one?
- What was the outcome of those initiatives?
- Does the current initiative deserve the cynicism it will encounter — or is it genuinely different in ways that can be demonstrated?
- Is the resistance this initiative will face rational given the learner's actual experience?

*Pass condition: The cynicism is sediment from prior experience that this initiative can honestly differentiate itself from. The differentiation is real, not just claimed.*

*Fail condition: The cynicism is warranted. The initiative genuinely resembles the ones that failed before it, for similar reasons. Training cannot excavate buried care in service of something that deserves the burial.*

*Cynicism Audit specific condition: If this question fails while Questions 1 and 2 pass — the initiative has genuine value and reasonable adoption conditions but is operating in poisoned organizational soil — the warranted intervention includes an explicit organizational trust-repair component before any substrate intervention is deployed. This is a specific design mode, not a full fail.*

---

## Warrant Outcomes

**Full Pass — all three questions answered honestly and affirmatively:**
Proceed to substrate mapping and full engine deployment.

**Partial Pass — one or two questions pass, one fails:**
Proceed with modified scope. Document which question failed and why. Design the intervention to address only what is genuinely warranted. Flag the failing condition to the client as a prerequisite for full deployment. The engine runs in limited mode against the warranted components only.

**Full Fail — two or more questions fail:**
Do not deploy the engine. The warranted response is one or more of the following:
- Return to the client with an honest account of what needs to change before training is appropriate
- Recommend a non-training intervention: communication, consultation, process redesign, co-design with the affected population
- Decline the engagement if the client is unwilling to address the failing conditions

---

## Documentation Requirements

The warrant assessment is documented and retained regardless of outcome.

**For full or partial passes:** The warrant documentation becomes part of the ethical disclosure layer for the engagement. It is the system's honest account of why this intervention was deployed.

**For full fails:** The warrant documentation records what was recommended instead and why. It is the system's honest account of why the engine was not deployed.

Both are discoverable. Both are the system showing its work.

---

## The Designer's Shield

The Intervention Warrant exists in the architecture as a structural requirement, not a personal judgment. When a client relationship produces pressure to proceed without warrant, the designer's response is:

*"The system requires an intervention warrant before we can proceed. Here's what we need to establish together."*

The gate is not the designer's opinion. It is the engine's requirement. This distinction is load-bearing at scale.

---

## Foundational Principle

A regulation is not the value. A regulation is the crystallized memory of a human cost.

The Intervention Warrant is checking whether that memory is still alive in the way the initiative is being framed — and whether the intervention will keep it alive in the learner.

Compliance training that has forgotten this is not training. It is organizational self-protection wearing the mask of learning.

## ALSAP Warrant Assessment — Preliminary
*Astellas ALSAP Template Record Module*

**Question 1 — Value Evidence:** Pass.
Patient safety reporting has a genuine, specific, human-level case. Real patients are protected by accurate asset-level safety data. The connection between the template and the patient is real, even if currently invisible to most learners. The regulatory requirement exists because safety signals visible at the asset level have historically been missed when reporting only at the product level. That human rationale is alive, traceable, and available to be made visible by the intervention.

**Question 2 — Adoption Legitimacy:** Conditional pass.
The template exists and is mandated. The question of whether organizational support, tooling, and process infrastructure is sufficiently in place for a motivated learner to actually succeed requires honest confirmation from the client before full deployment. This condition is flagged for pre-deployment verification.

**Question 3 — Cynicism Audit:** Conditional pass.
The organizational substrate carries significant sediment from prior initiatives. The cynicism this initiative will encounter is rational given the learner's actual experience — it is not a character flaw but a well-trained prior. However, ALSAP has genuine value that differentiates it from prior initiatives that failed. That differentiation must be demonstrated, not asserted. The Align phase carries significant design weight as a result.

**Warrant Outcome:** Proceed with full engine deployment, with specific design requirements:
- Organizational trust-repair component required in Align phase
- Honest, demonstrable differentiation from prior failed initiatives — not claimed, shown
- Adoption legitimacy confirmed with client before deployment
- Patient safety human rationale must be made visible and personal — not stated as bromide but experienced as real
